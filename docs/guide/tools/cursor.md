# Cursor

**Key:** `V` · [All tools](index.md)

The Cursor tool edits nothing: hover over the model to see the voxel position and color
in the status bar, and the layer under the cursor.

| Option | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Show names** | *On Hover* or *Always*: show the name of the layer under the cursor. |

Use it to look around a model without the risk of a stray click. Right-drag orbits and
middle-drag pans, as with every tool.

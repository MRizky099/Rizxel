# History

Lists the undo steps of the active document, oldest first. Click a row to go back to
that state; undone rows stay listed until you make a new edit, so clicking one redoes up
to it.

One drag is one step. *Settings ▸ General ▸ Undo steps per document* sets how many are
kept. `Ctrl+Z` undoes, `Ctrl+Y` or `Ctrl+Shift+Z` redoes.

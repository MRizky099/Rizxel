# Bound Model

Sets the size of the model area (the yellow wireframe), from 1 to 256 voxels per axis.
Shrinking the bounds removes the voxels outside them; it is undoable.

Other ways to change the bounds:

- The [Move](../tools/move.md) tool's *Resize* mode drags a face of the bounds.
- *Model ▸ Fit Bounds to Content* shrinks them to the voxels.
- *Model ▸ Fit Bounds on Import* fits imported models automatically.

# Tools and Options

The **Tools** panel (the rail on the left) holds the [tools](../tools/index.md), two mode
toggles and the Settings button. Hover a tool for its name and shortcut.

- **Slice** (`Space`): Slice Mode, one slice of the model with any tool. While it is
  on, its label shows the axis and the slice number (`Y 3/12`) in the axis colour.
- **Mask** (`Shift+M`): Mask Mode, the selected voxels are protected from every tool.

The **Options** panel (the option bar) holds, from left to right:

1. Undo and redo.
2. The voxel operation: **Add**, **Subtract**, **Join** or **Paint**
   ([details](../tools/index.md#voxel-operations)).
3. The active tool's name, its `?` guide link, and its options (see each tool's page).

In Render mode the bar holds the render controls instead: pause, a *Raster* or *Path
traced* preview, the preview resolution, the sample count, the output size and the
projection; its `?` opens [Render mode](../render-mode.md).

The bar scrolls sideways when the window is too narrow.

While docked on their own, both panels have a dotted grip instead of a tab: drag it to
move the panel, or right-click it to float, move or close it.
